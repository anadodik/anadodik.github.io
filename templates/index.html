Graphics & Society

Untangling how graphics impacts and is impacted by humans, namely its relationship to perception, interaction, and societal motivators and outcomes. PhD student and Social and Ethical Responsibilities of Computing (SERC) Scholar at MIT CSAIL, EECS.
